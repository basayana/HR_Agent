Business Expense Policy

The company reimburses reasonable, necessary, and pre-approved business expenses. Examples include client travel, approved training, and business meals.

Submit an expense claim in the expense portal within 30 days of the expense date. Attach itemized receipts for every expense above INR 500.

For domestic travel, use economy class unless a director has approved an exception in writing. Alcohol, personal purchases, traffic fines, and costs without a business purpose are not reimbursable.

Manager approval is required for claims up to INR 10,000. Claims above INR 10,000 also require finance approval.
