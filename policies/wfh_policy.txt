Work From Home (WFH) Policy

Eligible employees may work remotely up to two days each week, subject to manager approval and business needs. Agree regular remote days with your manager in advance.

Employees must be reachable during their normal working hours and attend required meetings. They must use the company VPN and follow all information-security requirements when working remotely.

Temporary exceptions, including fully remote work for a limited period, require written approval from the department head and HR.

Employees should maintain a safe, quiet work area with reliable internet. The company does not reimburse ordinary home internet or utility costs.
