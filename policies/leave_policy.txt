Leave Policy

Full-time employees receive 18 paid leave days per calendar year. Leave accrues at 1.5 days at the end of each completed month.

Employees should request planned leave through the HR portal at least five working days before the first day of absence. Managers approve requests based on team coverage.

Up to five unused paid leave days may be carried into the next calendar year. Carried-over leave must be used by 31 March or it expires.

For illness lasting more than two consecutive working days, submit a medical certificate to HR within three working days of returning to work.

Total paid leave days per year: employees are entitled to 33 leave days in total per calendar year, combining all leave types.
